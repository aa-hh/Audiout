# A nicer-sounding sync wizard: research and shaped options

2026-10-06. The owner finds the two sounds the sync wizard plays shrill and a
bad first impression, and asked what else could do the job given the Bluetooth
limits. Four research tracks, then a shaping pass that rendered and benched
concrete candidates. Everything below is in this folder; the listening files
are regenerated by the scripts (see "Re-running").

## Decisions so far (owner, 2026-10-06)

- **By-ear ticks: T1 mallet** (440 Hz on the Mac/AirPlay side, 660 Hz on the
  Bluetooth side, a fifth apart). Chosen by ear from `shaped/TICK-OPTIONS.md`.
  Open sub-choice: decay 135 ms as rendered, or a shorter 60–80 ms ring that
  separates the two hits better at offsets under 50 ms.
- **Probe: P1 (parallel low glides) rejected** as still too shrill. The owner
  likes the whoosh direction (P3). The probe no longer has to be 1 s: up to
  10 s is acceptable with a progress bar in the wizard.
- **Still open:** P5 versus P3R for the Mac lane, and the length (6 s just
  clears the floors, 10 s clears them comfortably). Listen to `p5_10s.wav`,
  `p3r_10s.wav`, `p5_6s.wav` (regenerate with `psy/probe_p5.py`).

## What the wizard plays today

Two separate stimuli, both heard in one run:

1. **Mic probe.** Two 1 s exponential sine sweeps at once: Mac lane DOWN
   2000 → 500 Hz, Bluetooth lane UP 3200 → 10 000 Hz. The arrival difference
   at one mic is the offset. `SyncProbeCorrelator.swift` in audiout-shared,
   staged by `AlignmentTickInjector.stageProbe`.
2. **By-ear ticks.** 30 ms clicks of two decaying sine partials: 1800 + 2900 Hz
   on the Bluetooth side, 900 + 1450 Hz on the Mac side. The user judges which
   sounded first.

## Findings that decide things

- **The Bluetooth sweep is the culprit.** Sharpness (Zwicker's shrillness
  measure, in acum) is 4.4 for that lane alone; the standard annoyance model
  starts penalising at 1.75. It is a lone sine at every instant and its first
  0.39 s sit in the ear's most sensitive band. The Mac lane scores 1.1 and the
  ticks 1.2–1.6: not harsh, just cheap-sounding. (`02-psychoacoustics.md`)
- **Moving both lanes below about 4.5 kHz removes the sharpness penalty**, at
  a measured cost of about 2 dB of confidence and no loss of accuracy.
- **Two speakers must keep disjoint frequency bands.** Every band-sharing
  trick (different noise seeds, Golay pairs, Zadoff-Chu, opposite sweep
  directions) separates by 21–35 dB at most, against the 23 dB level gap
  between the near Mac speaker and the far Bluetooth one. Benched, every
  shared-band candidate was refused or confidently wrong. (`03-signal-theory.md`,
  `04-experiments.md`)
- **A whoosh (band-limited pink noise with a slow swell) measures accurately
  but scores low.** Noise carries ~8 dB less energy than a sweep at equal
  loudness, and a finite noise burst's own ripple caps its peak-to-sidelobe
  score at roughly √(duration × bandwidth). On the narrow 300–1100 Hz Mac
  band that cap is 12 at 1 s and 32 at 10 s, below both apps' acceptance
  floors (Mac 20 at `MicProbeSession.swift:488`, phone 25 at
  `AlignmentRunController.swift:149`). On the 1500–4500 Hz Bluetooth band the
  whoosh reaches 46 at 4 s and clears the floors from about 6 s.
  **So: whoosh on the Bluetooth lane only; a sweep-type sound on the Mac
  lane.** That is P5/P3R. (`shaped/PROBE-OPTIONS.md`)
- **Musical sounds work under rules.** Any sound that fills its band gives the
  same timing sharpness as a sweep. The hazard is consonance: a chord or
  arpeggio puts a false peak a few ms from the true one (a C-E-G stab was
  wrong by >1 ms in 38 % of noisy trials). A semitone-stepped sweep is nearly
  free (false peak −16 dB); pentatonic is not (−4.5 dB). The correlator already
  computes `peakMargin` (height of the nearest rival) but neither app reads it.
- **Codecs don't matter.** AAC and SBC round trips changed no candidate's
  timing or confidence.
- **Longer helps as √duration**, for sweeps and for the whoosh ceiling alike.
  Repeating an identical 1 s swell and taking the median does not raise the
  score. Today's 1 s probe at 3 s goes 31 → 50 in bench units.
- **No shipping product uses a musical or branded measurement sound.** Sonos
  Trueplay plays brown noise under a falling sweep for 45 s (patent
  US9736584) and measures several speakers in turn (US11337017). Apple TV's
  iPhone audio sync is the closest analogue and Apple publishes nothing about
  its tone. (`01-competitors.md`)

## Build items a long whoosh probe needs (from `shaped/BUILD-COST.md`)

1. **Noise generator in ProbeKit.** The phone recreates the probe locally, so
   the whoosh must come out identical on Mac and iPhone and at three sample
   rates (44.1 k playback, the Mac mic's rate, the phone's rate). Today's sweep
   is a formula in time and survives this; a per-sample seeded generator does
   not. BUILD-COST.md gives two ways (define the noise at one fixed rate and
   resample, or define it in the frequency domain). Never rely on Swift's
   built-in random-to-Double conversion, which may change between releases.
2. **Noise-weighting smoothing.** The correlator divides by the ambient
   spectrum smoothed over 64 FFT bins (`SyncProbeCorrelator.swift:541`). A
   longer probe means a bigger FFT, so 64 bins cover fewer Hz and the estimate
   gets ragged, which caps any lane longer than ~4 s (P5's Mac sweep: 147 at
   2 s, 54 at 6 s; with weighting off it rises to 318). Set the smoothing to a
   fixed width in Hz.
3. **Rival-peak guard (P3R only).** The repeat puts a near-copy of the true
   peak exactly 1 s away (0.9 dB under at 10 s). Either limit the lag search
   to under 1 s around the expected arrival, or vary the run between repeats.
   The bench's search window is ~1.1 s so it did not see this; the real
   recording does.
4. **Acceptance floors were set from sweep data.** Re-derive from the new
   signal's false-match scores before shipping; a false match scored 55 live
   with today's sweep.
5. **Ships as a package tag + two pin bumps** (AGENTS.md rule in
   audiout-shared); there is nothing on the wire saying which sound is
   playing, so a Mac and phone on different versions would correlate
   mismatched templates and report a confident wrong number.
6. **Sweep length is hand-copied in three places** (`ProbeAnalyzer.sweepSeconds`,
   `AlignmentTickInjector.probeSweepSeconds`, and the phone); the 2 s stagger in
   the phone's run must stay longer than the probe.
7. **Wizard UI:** progress bar for a 6–10 s probe; copy that describes the
   sound ("a soft rushing sound") where the Mac's copy currently names ticks.

## Tick build items (Mac only, S effort)

- `AlignmentTickInjector.renderTick` and the two partial tables; the
  loudness trim should move from A-weighting to an ISO 532-1 figure (the
  current −1.28 dB should be −1.60 dB for today's pair; the mallet pair needs
  −0.77 dB).
- The same tick is the row's "Play ticks" metronome and the phone's by-ear
  session (the phone never renders ticks, it only asks the Mac to play them).
- The by-ear estimator was tuned on today's click; an identical envelope on
  both sides is what keeps the stored latency unbiased.
- `dev/notes/wizard-tick-stimulus-brief.md` credits a finding to Szymaszek;
  the linked paper is Fostick, Lifshitz-Ben-Basat and Babkoff (2019).

## Re-running

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install numpy scipy mosqito soundfile
cd bench && ../.venv/bin/python bench.py --only=P5_10s,P3R10 --noise-shape=step12 --seeds=10
```

The psy scripts expect a `venv/` beside them and write .wav files next to
their own location; read each script's header for its exact paths, which were
written for the scratchpad layout and may need the two output directories
created first.

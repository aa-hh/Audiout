# What paid Mac indie apps do when the trial ends (survey, 2026-09-26)

Research only. Sources are vendor pages unless marked otherwise. Where a vendor page and a third-party page disagree, the vendor page wins and the conflict is noted. "Unverified" means no source could be found for that cell, not that the answer is "no".

Almost no vendor documents the exact screen shown at expiry, the button order, or whether a price appears on that screen. Those columns are mostly "unverified". Confirming them would need hands-on installs with the clock moved forward.

## Synthesis

**No app collects email at trial start** among the direct-download utilities where this could be checked. Rogue Amoeba states it outright ([vendor](https://rogueamoeba.com/support/knowledgebase/?showArticle=Misc-AboutAppTrials&product=General)). Apps that need an account (Sketch, Affinity, Boom 3D) or an Apple ID (App Store builds) are the exceptions.

**Menu-bar and background utilities split three ways.**

A hard stop at a date: BetterTouchTool (app shows disabled, developer refuses in-app resets), Keyboard Maestro (every macro refuses to run, big alert on opening the editor), Bartender ("will prompt you to purchase"), iStat Menus (EULA says functionality is disabled). None of the four vendors documents whether the menu-bar icon stays, and none documents the screen itself.

Keep running with a permanent limit and no end date: Rogue Amoeba (noise after 10 or 20 minutes per session), Little Snitch (filter switches off every 3 hours, user switches it back on), Hazel (drops to one folder and two rules), PopClip (nags after 250 actions). These four keep working after the trial in some form. Among the audio utilities, the Rogue Amoeba products are the only ones with a documented model; Sound Control, Boom 3D and eqMac document none.

A free tier the trial falls back to: Proxyman, TablePlus, Fantastical, Bear, eqMac, Reeder, Alfred, Rocket. Here the trial unlocks a paid tier and expiry returns the user to a working free app.

Among non-menu-bar document apps, **read-only after expiry** is common: Things, Ulysses (export kept on), Soulver, Sketch (90 days, then deletion). Things adds a purchase link inside the trial and keeps the data.

**The Rogue Amoeba model.** The trial has no calendar end. Every app is fully featured until a per-session usage threshold passes, then noise is laid over the audio; quitting and relaunching (or toggling the device, or restarting the stream) starts a fresh clean session. The vendor calls this a "free and fair trial" that lets users "confirm that the software does what you need prior to purchase", with "all features and functions available in trial mode" ([vendor](https://rogueamoeba.com/support/knowledgebase/?showArticle=Misc-AboutAppTrials&product=General)). Airfoil, the Rogue Amoeba product that sends audio to AirPlay speakers, uses the same model at 10 minutes of streaming. Buying sends a personal key the user pastes into the License window; no automatic unlock link is documented.

**The unlimited-with-nag model.** Sublime Text: "There is currently no enforced time limit for the evaluation" ([vendor](https://www.sublimetext.com/download)); a nag window appears at random on save ([third-party analysis](https://zerosum0x0.blogspot.com/2016/09/removing-sublime-text-nag-window.html)). Dash: Kapeli said in 2013 that the free version "allows everything" and only nags ([Kapeli on X](https://twitter.com/kapeli/status/384140298399797248)); the current form of the nag is unverified. Fork works the same way per third-party reports. Neither Sublime HQ nor Kapeli has published conversion figures for this model that could be found.

**"What you'll lose" or usage summary on the expired screen.** No vendor page describes one. The closest is Tower's Windows release notes: the expired screen "shows the available next steps" and offers "recovery options" ([vendor](https://www.git-tower.com/release-notes)); contents unverified. Tower's Mac trial badge was changed to state days left "plainly instead of pushing an upgrade message" and can be hidden (same source, 17.2).

**Menu-bar icon alive after expiry.** Documented for Little Snitch (it raises an alert each time the filter switches off and lets the user switch it back on), the Rogue Amoeba apps (they never stop running), Hazel (demo mode keeps running) and PopClip (keeps working with a nag). No surveyed app is documented as quitting itself and leaving the menu bar at expiry. The hard-stop apps (BetterTouchTool, Keyboard Maestro, Bartender, iStat Menus) are undocumented on this point; the BetterTouchTool forum screenshot describes the app still open but disabled.

**Unlock methods.** Paste a key: Rogue Amoeba, Little Snitch, Sublime Text, Default Folder X. Unlock without typing: Panic (button in the receipt email), PopClip and Moom (double-click a license file), Proxyman (an `activate?key=` link). App Store and subscription apps unlock automatically.

**Trial resets.** Panic reset every Nova trial when a major version shipped ([vendor on X](https://x.com/panic/status/1348730301741035520)). Things support grants extensions when expiry is wrong ([vendor](https://culturedcode.com/things/support/articles/2803551/)). BetterTouchTool's developer declines in-app resets. No app was found offering a self-serve "extend my trial" button on the expired screen.

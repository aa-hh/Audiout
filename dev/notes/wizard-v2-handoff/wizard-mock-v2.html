Two Voices, on the Plate

The stage becomes a fixed dark instrument plate — an inset screen that never themes, taking over half the window. The two lights speak the brand's two secondaries: Sync Green for the speaker being aligned, Party Magenta for the reference — both gamut-chosen to stay electric on near-black, and exact complements, so their additive fusion is literally white. Gold stays the primary voice (CTAs, detent, chrome). Each light spills softly into the room, so the whole window leans with the belief. Below the plate: two big tactile plates (drawn, hardware-feeling, fully stock behavior underneath), one quiet bar, two corner exits. One line of text on the entire screen.

A real state ladder replaces the smooth lerp. Four confidence rungs (open >250 ms · closing ≤250 · near ≤60 · nearly-locked ≤12), each an unmistakably distinct look. Promotion = a camera push-in with a mechanical detent — a ratchet click of banked progress. A mistake = a slower, softer pull-back that never brightens. The ambiguity you saw is structurally gone.

Two voices, additive fusion. Sync Green #2BFF8F (your speaker) + Party Magenta #FF90E9 (the reference) — the website's own pair, exact complements, both at peak brightness on near-black. Their meeting makes white, brighter than either. The lock is a 1.6 s sequence: quiet → rings merge with a soft collision → the wire's light gathers into the ring from both sides → contract → one white bloom → settle. Doctrine note: this is the group-identity exception (color names which speaker, never state) — "is it live" stays gold everywhere else.

The plate never themes. Fixed near-black in both appearances — in light mode it's a dark screen set into a light chassis, like hardware. This also fixes two latent light-mode bugs (the glow that measured 1.78:1, themed stamps on the stage).

One line of text on the question screen. The interval caption under the plate — that's it. Reference picker moves to intro only (changing it mid-run restarts the run anyway); the clock and "usually ~15 clicks" live in the nameplate corner; "Which speaker clicked first?" is carried by the plates themselves.

Plates are drawn, behavior is stock. Custom-drawn NSButton cells (the WarmFaderCell pattern): press inverts the bevel, arrow keys visibly depress the plate, focus rings/VoiceOver/keyboard all native. Every color token ships with light + dark + Increase Contrast variants and measured contrast.

Locked ring goes warm-white #FFF4E2 with the value stamped beneath — neither voice "wins"; the pair resolves into the brand's warmth. The fused-state gradient between the lights passes through a pale desaturated band, never through a third hue (complements can't accidentally make red).

The pick

As One, on the Rail

C's two lights become the two ends of the credible interval, riding on one wire. The gap between them is the uncertainty; the lit span of the wire between them is the interval — the lights and the rail are one mechanism, not two. They can meet, but never cross: separation is the interval's width, and a width can't go negative. At fusion they arrive concentric, then lock into one ring.

Crossing is solved by the mapping. The lights aren't "the two speakers moving toward each other" — they're the two ends of what the run still can't rule out. Ends of a range meet; they never pass through each other. At fusion they sit concentric (gold ring inside warm-white ring), then become one.

Blur and distance can't disagree. Both are driven by the same number — the interval's width. Softer always means farther apart, crisper always means closer. One truth, two expressions.

The rail is native, not bolted on. The wire is the stage floor; the lit span between the lights is the interval — same one-wire language as the popover's connection rail. Faint scale ticks slide outward as the stage zooms in (A-zoom), so precision is felt as the ruler stretching.

The swing is the pair's position. Where the pair sits along the wire is the current lean. A contradicting answer slides the pair sideways and eases it apart-and-softer over ~600 ms — interesting weather, never a penalty.

The lights are alive but honest. Each breathes slowly, out of phase with the other — ambient, music-like. The breathing's mood follows the run's real tempo class (searching = slow, close quarters = quicker) without ever claiming to pulse on the beat, which the AirPlay buffer forbids. Reduce Motion: breathing off, states crossfade.

The lock is two-stage. Proposal: the fused soft ring breathes while you listen ("Here's the alignment — listen"). "Sounds right": the ring snaps crisp with one contained bloom, the lit span collapses into it, and the value stamps beneath. Camera-focus lock, not confetti.

A

The Ridge

The estimator's actual belief curve, drawn as a luminous gold ridge over a baseline wire between the two speakers. Every answer reflows it — it leans toward whichever speaker is early, sharpens as it narrows, softens and spreads when an answer contradicts. The math itself is the spectacle; nothing is faked.

The zoom is the drama. The stage rescales as confidence tightens — early answers move mountains, the endgame is a close-up on a few ms. Homing in is felt as a camera closing in.

Rail kinship. The lit span of the baseline wire is the credible interval — same one-wire language as the popover's connection rail.

Mistakes read as weather, not failure: the ridge eases wider over ~600 ms. No lurch, no red.

Risk: the opening flat ridge is visually quiet — the armed state needs a deliberate "wide open" look so the first answers feel like sculpting.

B

The Needle

A pan-pot for time. A damped needle on a wide dial arc leans toward whichever speaker is early; behind it, a fan of light whose spread is the uncertainty. Answers narrow the fan into a beam; the needle settles inside it. The most mixing-desk of the three — this is an instrument you'd find on the console.

Heavily damped by design. The needle carries mass — it drifts, never snaps. The election-needle failure was jitter without annotation; here motion only ever means "belief moved", and the words below say so.

The range ring shows the zoom. A faint outer scale relabels as the dial rescales from ±900 ms toward ±10 ms, so precision is visible as the dial "gearing down".

Detent finale. The lock is mechanical: fan collapses to a beam, needle drops into a notch. Deeply satisfying, zero confetti.

Risk: a needle summarizes — the median leans, but skew and lumpy belief are invisible. Least honest of the three about what the estimator actually knows.

C

As One

Two soft discs of light, one per speaker. Their separation is the believed offset; their blur is the uncertainty. Answers slide them together and pull them into focus, until they fuse into one crisp ring — which is exactly what you're listening for. The goal is drawn, so the stage explains itself; the condensed brief barely has a job.

Meaning matches the ear. "The clicks should land as one" is drawn literally: when the lights become one, so do the clicks. The proposal screen needs almost no words.

Calmest of the three — no chart, no axis, no instrument to read. Two lights breathing toward each other in a dark room. Peak "Instrument in a Dark Room".

Mistakes are a refocus: the lights drift apart slightly and soften. Reads as "hmm, let's look again", never as a penalty.

Risk: least informative — the belief's shape and skew are invisible, and separation must be log-scaled or the endgame (a few ms) shows nothing moving.

Common to all three

The chassis

Everything below ships regardless of which stage wins. It's where the research earned its keep.

Dedicated floating window (~560×380), opened from the row's Align action. Esc stops; closing cancels safely — the session already guarantees the tick dies and the prior value returns on any teardown.

Armed state replaces the intro screen. The same stage, dormant: one line ("Tap the speaker you hear first"), the reference picker, Start. No separate briefing card to read past.

The plates are the speakers. Real device names as the two big answer targets, keycaps ←/→, Space for "They sound together", ⌘Z Back kept quiet. Same locked answer model, same session, new skin.

Status in words, ms in one caption. "Still wide → narrowing → close now → nearly locked." The tempo shift to quick clicks at 250 ms gets its own beat: "Close — quick clicks now."

Frugality stated up front: "usually ~15 clicks" (SoundID's trick — each answer feels high-leverage).

No per-answer verdicts. Never right/wrong, never red (Apple's hearing test). Backward swings ease over ~600 ms and get words, not drama — the election-needle lesson.

No visual metronome. The AirPlay path buffers 1–2 s and there's no beat callback, so nothing on screen pretends to pulse with the clicks. The stage reacts to answers only.

Reduce Motion: morphs become crossfades; the finale becomes a fade-in of the locked state.

Bow-outs and kept screens restyled in the same chrome with today's locked plain-speech copy.

Cost honesty: new window + the session exposing a belief snapshot (the posterior already holds it). Estimator, flow, and audio path untouched.

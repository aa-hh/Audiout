Direction C: rear panel of the desk

Settings drops its sidebar and lays every setting out at once, the way the back of an amplifier shows every socket. The left column follows the sound (Source, then Output); the right column holds the desk's own controls (Power, Control, Appearance); the plate across the bottom says what this copy of Audiout is and what it sends home.

All frames are 1x, 713 pt wide. Stock switches, radios and the slider are drawn in the grey that the shipped renders show; in a key window they take the Mac's accent colour, as they do in every direction. The QR tile is a stand-in pattern and does not scan.

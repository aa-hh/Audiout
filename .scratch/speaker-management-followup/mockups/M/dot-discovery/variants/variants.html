Sidebar reachability marks

Fourteen ways to mark a speaker row in the Speakers tab sidebar as reachable on the network or not. Filled means reachable (owner, 2026-10-04). No ring, no red, no gold, and the two states differ in shape, never in colour alone.

Held constant

210 pt sidebar, 28 pt rows, 13 pt names, device icons about 16 pt in a 22 pt box. Real SF Symbols, rendered from the system.

A row the Mac can't reach dims its name and icon to labelCool2, the Mixer's "Unavailable" ink (critique problem 6).

Selected rows

The mark takes the row's text colour: label on the grey pill (11.8 light, 7.4 dark), white on the accent pill (4.8 light, 6.4 dark). No tinted ink survives the accent pill: rim measures 1.04 light and 1.37 dark on it. So on a selected row, shape is the only signal.

Grounds are estimates

Nobody has measured the source-list ground since 2026-09-03. Ratios run over light #E8E8EA to #F5F5F6 and dark #1E1E20 to #2C2C2E; strips draw #EEEEEF and #232325. Underlined figures fall under the 3:1 floor for marks.

One shape, five inks

Option 1's disc and dash in each candidate colour, on the estimated sidebar grounds. rim is the Mixer's connected colour (HaloRingView.swift:243-249). labelCool2 is the Mixer's "Unavailable" ink (DeviceRowView.swift:1177). Mixing them (rim disc, labelCool2 dash) inverts in dark: the dash would be brighter than the disc.

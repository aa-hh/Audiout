Direction A · Speakers twin

Settings takes the Speakers tab's sidebar shape and page grammar whole. The License gets the slot Main Audio has on Speakers: one plate under a title at the top of the sidebar, with the sections listed under "Settings". Every page is a page header over outlined cards of rows, and every row carries the control that changes it in its trailing slot, with a caption that says what the current value does.

  • Header strip copied from the Speakers render, with the Settings tab engaged and named. Surfaces are 713 pt at 1x.
  • Blue on switches and the slider is the Mac's own accent colour on stock controls in a key window (it follows System Settings). The shipped headless renders show these grey.
  • No Settings hue: neutral by design (brief, open decision 1). Gold appears only on the existing theme-tile selection ring and inside the tile previews.
  • The QR tile is drawn for illustration and does not scan. "Sam's iPhone" is a sample name.
  • Three strings are new and need a yes: the trial caption on the License key row, the "Audiout" sidebar title, and the License header captions built from existing lines.

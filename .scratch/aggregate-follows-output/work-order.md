### Goal

Make the public "Audiout" aggregate wrap the output the user was listening through when Audiout took the Mac's default (headphones in the jack, a USB DAC, a display, AirPods or a Bluetooth speaker), not "the first built-in output". When that device disappears mid-session, re-point the aggregate onto the Mac's first built-in output (headphones if present, else speakers, the order macOS falls back in); when it comes back, move the aggregate back onto it. A Bluetooth device that is also being fed by its own Bluetooth row is never wrapped (it would get two feeds). Keep the two local playback paths (the delayed "This Mac" copy and per-app audio redirected to the Mac) landing on the same device, and rebuild the whole-system tap after a re-point so it is not left pinned to a vanished device. Fixes ticket 06, silence after unplug, and the Mac's copy playing on the speakers while headphones are in.

### Decisions (settled)

- **Wrappable** = resolves now AND `kAudioDevicePropertyTransportType` ∈ {`BuiltIn`, `USB`, `HDMI`, `DisplayPort`, `Thunderbolt`, `PCI`, `FireWire`, `AVB`, `Bluetooth`, `BluetoothLE`} (`kAudioDeviceTransportType…` constants). Excludes AirPlay, every aggregate (ours and other builds'), virtual devices. Swap noted: narrower and cheaper than routing through `isLoopRisk`.
- **Bluetooth conflict rule:** a candidate UID that is in `btSelectedUIDs` or `btPerAppClaimedUIDs` is treated as not wrappable (aggregate falls back to the first built-in output). Re-reconciled at both write sites of those lists.
- **Wanted sub-device, one rule:** candidate = `priorDefaultUID` while `aggregateDefaultActive`, else current default UID; wanted = candidate if wrappable and not Bluetooth-fed, else `builtInOutputDeviceUID()`. Applied at launch, at takeover (before the set-default write), on every device-list change, on every Bluetooth selection change.
- **Move back on return: yes.** Repair in place, never destroy+create. Liveness = the wanted UID resolves (no active-sub-device-list read; razor ceiling, live check confirms).
- **Telemetry:** local-only `aggregate_subdevice` with `reason` ∈ {`launch`, `takeover`, `device_list`, `bt_selection`}, `outcome` ∈ {`set`, `unchanged`, `failed`, `no_target`}, `from`, `to`, `now`. No PostHog.
- **Bluetooth latency:** out of scope to correct; the live check measures the lag and a follow-up decides whether to feed the Bluetooth device's measured latency into the local sink's reference.

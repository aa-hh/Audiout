Speakers tab as the work order builds it

M/FINAL and M/FINAL-GREEN-2 combined, with work-order-M.md applied wherever they differ. Step numbers refer to that work order. Windows at the real 713 pt width. The 20 speakers are the owner's fleet; which speaker is which kind is illustrative.

1 · After the search · Overview selected, sidebar focused (steps 7–12, 15, 20–22)

Every number adds up. The 8 rows above the dividers are Available: 4 AirPlay, 0 Bluetooth, 3 Cast, 1 This Mac. The dividers read 8 and 4, so Unavailable is 12, and the header's 20 is every speaker row. Green only on Available, the counts above 0 and the plus on Pair Bluetooth speaker. The sidebar is drawn at full length; the app scrolls it after about 10 speakers. The Add scene bar is one row tall with no line above it.

The 6 that can’t be found are ION Speaker, isaac, Missing speaker, Onkyo TX-8220, Sonos Move (SONOS Kitchen) and teevee. Their rows carry no mark; the tooltip, VoiceOver, their page and the Forget sheet name them. The other 6 unavailable are Bluetooth speakers macOS still lists.

2 · Overview states · content pane only, drawn at 72 % (steps 18, 20–23)

3a · Sonos Move, curve shaped · just clicked in the sidebar, so the blue pill

Green on the status word Ready (step 26) and on the equalizer mark, which stays. Bass 3 dB and Reset show only while the curve is shaped. Show in Mixer: “Shown while your Mac can reach it.” (step 27).

3b · TV, curve flat · focus moved into the page, so the grey pill

Green on Connected only. A flat curve shows Flat, with no mark and no Reset.

3c · isaac, on the can’t-be-found list · 10 s or more after launch (steps 25–26)

The caption leads with the question-mark glyph in the quieter grey; nothing on the line is green. The editor is hidden. The stored tone shows as the title-line summary and the kept note, and Forget “isaac”… sits below. The mark is the equalizer’s own green, which follows the stored tone as the code does today. Show in Mixer: “Shown even when your Mac can’t reach it.”

3d · The same speaker, before the list fills · 5 s after launch

“AirPlay Speaker · Unavailable” with no glyph and no Forget. The stored tone and the kept note still show, and the Show in Mixer card moves up under the note (step 26). The third caption, “Shown only while it’s in use.”, belongs to a speaker set to Hide when not in use, like Fancyy.

4 · Main Audio page in cool greys · step 28

The Equalizer title, slider labels, readouts and note move from warm greys to cool ones. No green on this page. No line under the title. Reset stays on the title line and is dimmed while the curve is flat.

5 · Selected rows on both highlights · step 11

Every ink in a selected row follows the pill: white on the blue pill while the list has focus, the normal label colour on the grey pill. A selected unreachable row stays under its divider. A Shift range skips the divider, so TV and ION Speaker read as two pills (step 14).

6 · Forget from the keyboard or the menu · eight unreachable rows selected (steps 13, 32)

⌘⌫ with the sidebar focused, or the menu item, forgets only the selected speakers that can’t be found: 6 of the 8. JBL Flip 5 and Move 2 (SONOS Bedroom) are skipped because macOS still lists them. If none of the selection is on the list, no sheet opens. The sheet names two speakers, then “and N more”; Missing speaker has no saved details and is never named. Cancel takes Return. No Forget anywhere in the first 10 s after launch.

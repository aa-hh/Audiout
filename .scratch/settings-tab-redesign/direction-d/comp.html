Direction D · Status spine

Each Settings sidebar row is the Speakers sidebar's two-line row: the section name, then a readout of what is set there. General's three stateful topics split out so every readout states one fact. Dark left, light right, 1x, 713 pt surface. Switches and the selection pill are drawn in their non-key grey, as in the reference renders; with the panel key they take the Mac's accent colour. Readouts are neutral (labelCool): no Settings hue is proposed.

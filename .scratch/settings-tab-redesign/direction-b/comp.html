Settings, direction B: one Mixer page, no sidebar

Four cards with the Mixer's card headers on one scrolling page. Chevrons, card titles, row names and the 200 pt trailing column sit at the Mixer's exact x positions, so flipping between the Mixer and Settings moves nothing. The folds are the only way around the page. Settings stays neutral: the only gold is the shipped theme-tile ring and, if Alec agrees, the reused fader's idle readout. Switches and radios are stock and take the Mac's own accent colour; they are drawn in graphite because the renders show that. Row glyphs are hand-drawn stand-ins for the SF Symbols named in the brief. The QR pattern is a stand-in. All sizes are 1x points.

1. Full page

Registered licence, iPhone control on with no phones yet, a Mac with a Touch Bar, Advanced closed. Drawn at full length; the dashed line is 711 pt, the height the Mixer and Speakers renders open at. Below it the page scrolls under the header strip.

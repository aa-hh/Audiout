Scenes, direction C: console plates

Each scene is a plate built from the Mixer's own parts: the 48 pt icon well with its magenta glow, the name in the page-header voice, and the members on a short run of the membership rail. Surface 713 pt, drawn at 1 pt = 1 px.
Overview, dark. Party is under the pointer.
Two 336 pt columns; each plate drops into whichever column is shorter, so the 8-speaker plate and the 2-speaker plate never leave a hole. Downstairs is what Main Audio plays to: gold well edge, gold rail, label ink, "Playing" in its caption. Nothing on the plate starts playback.
Overview, light. Whole House has keyboard focus.
Light keeps the chassis flat: plates are a containerEdge outline on the paper ground. "Add scene" is shown in its hover state. Focus is the stock system focus ring plus the 0.10 hover wash, never gold.
Editor, dark. The Downstairs plate opened into the page.
Same well, same name position, same rail: the plate's header becomes the page header, and its compact member run becomes the full membership card with every speaker. Delete moves into an outlined row like a speaker page's Forget row.
Editor, light.

Empty state, dark.
No dashed tile and no invented plate. The Speakers page's outlined list explains the two ways a scene gets made; the header's "Add scene" is the only button.
Main Audio destination menu in the Mixer, scene entries.
Each scene entry carries its well's icon and the plate's caption as a second line, the two-line entry App Routing's menu already draws. The tick, not gold, marks the current destination: this menu is where a scene gets played, and the Scenes tab only shows the result.

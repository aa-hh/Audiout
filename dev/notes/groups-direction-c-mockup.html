Direction C — Devices own the sidebar; groups become an overview page

The sidebar is now one thing only: the fleet. Groups move out of the list and into the content pane as a card field you read at a glance — who is in each group, and which one is live.

1  Groups overview. The pinned Groups row at the top of the sidebar is the only non-device row; selecting it fills the pane with the group cards. Gold on the row and on the card is the same signal: something is playing.

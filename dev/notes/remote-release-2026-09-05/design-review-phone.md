**One module, two adapters.** The revised row's "one recorder port with a PostHog adapter and an in-memory adapter" is the right shape. Concretely: one type with an `enum Event` carrying the nine cases and their properties from `analytics-events.md` (`intro:card_seen`, `intro:find_mac_tapped`, `connect:connected`, `demo:entered`, `sync:opened`, `sync:measure_tapped`, `sync:verdict`, `sync:recheck_accepted`, `sync:by_ear_nudged`), one `capture(_:)`, consent read inside from `UserDefaults` with the default on (D12), and an injected sink. Deletion test: without it nine call sites each read consent and name the SDK. The Mac's `Analytics.swift` is in `AudioutCore` (GPL): retype the shape, never paste it (`AGENTS.md`, licence rule). Never hand the SDK `ClientIdentity` (`ConnectionController.swift:16-40`); it is the Mac's approval key. Let the SDK mint its own anonymous id.

**`mac_id` and D12, a consent gap.** D12 says the Mac sends its anonymous install id on `welcome` so a funnel can join the two devices. On the Mac that install id is its PostHog distinct id (`mac AppDelegate.swift:106-107` sets it as the anonymous id, `:2245` identifies with it), and the Mac's analytics are opt-in and off by default. If the phone forwards it as `mac_id` on its default-on stream, the Mac's PostHog identity leaves through a channel the Mac's user never opted into, and the phone's "not linked to you" label is stretched by an identifier PostHog can already join. Two fixes, either one: the Mac sends a `serverID` that is not its distinct id (a separate random id kept in `AppSettings`, or a keyed hash of the install id), or the Mac sends `serverID` only while its own consent is on. Decide before T2 tags, since `serverID` is a `welcome` field.

This is also why the Waves note "T7 and T11 in parallel (disjoint files)" is false: T11's type must exist before T7 wires its six events, and `RootView`/`AppSessionModel` events overlap T5 and T6. Land the type in W1; each task wires its own events.

## 2. Design it twice: the sync sheet's model for T7

### The problem space

One module has to own the sheet's flow from open to close: which act is showing and what its one gold action is, the Mac's clicks (on for the merged listen page and the by-ear page, off before Measure and before Clear, never left on), one run at a time through the per-run controller, the verdict and what it replaced, the tapped re-check and its "Not now", the chain to the next speaker, a refused side command shown as one line, keep-awake, and the moments the finger and VoiceOver are told about. Constraints any interface must meet: the phone renders alignment state and never computes it (`SyncSheet.swift:28-31`); one alignment handler at a time, claimed on open and released on close (`MacSessionProtocol.swift:95-98`); ticks off before Clear (`:1173-1178`) and before the sheet goes (`:218-219`); no run without a tap, and no run without the Hold still page (D10); sound only from the speakers being synced; Measure live whenever both play; the 2 s and 4 s of the Mac's demo are the phone's own clock; and the sheet's external interface, `SyncSheet(targetID:session:)` with two hosts (`DeviceRowView.swift:474`, `SyncInviteCard.swift:98`), does not change.

Dependencies per DEEPENING.md. The session is remote but owned (category 3) and already a port, `MacSessionProtocol`, with two production adapters (`RemoteSession`, `DemoMacSession`) and two preview fakes (`ConnectGateView.swift:932`, `SpeakersView.swift:947`); a scripted test fake is the fifth conformer and costs twenty empty methods, which the two previews already pay. The microphone, both its permission and one recording, is a true external (category 4) reached today through statics (`ProbeCaptureSession.permissionGranted`, `permissionDenied`, `permissionUndetermined`, `requestPermission()`) and through `ProbeSession()` constructed inside `AlignmentRunController.start()` (`:177`); the demo is branched inline at `:165-169` and `:234-236`. Two adapters are justified on day one, the real one and the canned one the demo already implies, so this is a real seam, and today it is a hypothetical one wearing an `if`. Time is category 4 with two precedents in this repo: injected durations (`ProbeSession.init(pipelineTail:timeout:)`, `:108-114`) and a clock protocol (`CommandClock`, `CommandSender.swift:10-17`, `FakeClock` at `RemoteSessionTests.swift:135`). Keep-awake, haptics and announcements are platform side effects; the model returns facts and one modifier applies them. ProbeKit is in-process and untouched.

### Design 1, minimal interface

`SyncSheetModel` with `state` and `send(_ intent:)`; init takes the target, the session, an optional `RoomListening` port (permission plus one recording with `sweepsStarted`, `sweepsFinished`, `cancel`) and a `Timings` value with `.instant` for tests; `Refusal` moves up from the controller; the controller stays internal and gains a completion closure; `state` is recomputed per read from the snapshot. Strong: the smallest surface, one port for the whole microphone, the `Timings` shape matches the repo's own precedent, and a computed `state` cannot drift from the snapshot. Thin: `Intent` is an enum with as many cases as Design 3 has methods, so the caller learns the same list spelled differently; moving `Refusal` is churn with no consumer outside the model; and "recomputed per read" cannot fire an event (a haptic counter, an announcement) on a snapshot change, so those need another mechanism.

### Design 2, maximum flexibility

`SyncRunModel` with `step`, `cues`, `begin`, `end`, `send`; `Act` carries `actions: [Action]` with a rank, so one `ActView` renders every act including refusals; a pure `static step(for: RunState, in: Snapshot?)`; a `forSheet(targetID:session:)` factory that is the only `isDemo` read; two ports (`MicrophoneAccess`, a measuring factory) and `Timings`; `AlignmentRunController.start` becomes `run() async -> Outcome`. Strong: the prompt-as-data shape covers the four pre-run pages and the refusal page with one view, the ranks map one to one onto `GoldCTALabel` and `TextAction`'s tones, the pure `step` makes every page rule assertable with no view, and the factory confines the demo branch to one line. Thin: four ranks where two survive the merged page (the neutral chips are gone), a `cues` struct that duplicates what `step` already implies, two ports where one thing varies, and `run() async` still needs an observable phase for the run page, so it is not simpler than a completion. Its claim that `playAlignmentDemo` must become audible is wrong (T6).

### Design 3, common caller first

`SyncRitual` with `stage`, `hooks`, `notice`; named intent methods with `advance()` as the one gold action on every prompt; ports `MicrophoneAccess` and `RoomProbe`; a `sleep` closure; a `.syncRitual(_:)` modifier bundling `begin`/`end`, `sensoryFeedback`, announcements and keep-awake. Strong: the highest leverage at the view, where one modifier and one gold-button rule replace forty lines of `.onChange`, `.sensoryFeedback`, `.task` and `.onDisappear` (`SyncSheet.swift:181-224`); the one-action-per-prompt invariant is enforced by the type; it found the `macBlocker` defect. Thin: fourteen methods is the widest surface of the three; a `sleep` closure is a third time shape in a repo that has two; `toggleTicks` and `measureAnyway` are intents the brief deletes.

### Comparison

**Depth.** Design 1 wins at the model's interface (four members once `begin` and `end` are counted). Design 3 wins at the view's (one modifier, one gold rule). Design 2 wins at the test's (a pure page derivation). None of the three wins all three, and nothing forces a choice: the pure derivation is the implementation of Design 1's computed `state`, and the modifier is an adapter over Design 1's facts.

**Locality.** All three put the flow in one file and the copy behind it. Design 2 moves the button ranks into the model, which is where they belong: "at most one gold action per page" becomes a property of a value the tests can iterate, not a rule the reviewer checks by eye. Design 1's `Refusal` move spreads a type that has one consumer. Design 3's `hooks` split the haptic facts from `stage`; Design 1 folds them into `state`, which keeps one read.

**Seam placement.** One port for the microphone (Design 1) over two (Designs 2 and 3): permission and recording never mix adapters, real pairs with real and canned with canned, so a second seam is one that nothing varies across. Injected durations (Designs 1 and 2) over a sleep closure (Design 3): the repo's precedent. The controller stays internal in all three; Design 1's completion closure over Design 2's `run() async`, because the run page needs the phase stream either way. Design 2's factory as the only `isDemo` read; Design 3's modifier as the only place UIKit and the haptic engine are touched.

### Recommendation: Design 1's interface, Design 2's prompt, Design 3's modifier

One model, `SyncSheetModel`, `@MainActor @Observable`, built by `SyncSheet.init` through a factory that reads `session.isDemo` once:

```swift
static func forSheet(targetID: String, session: any MacSessionProtocol) -> SyncSheetModel
init(targetID: String, session: any MacSessionProtocol, room: any RoomListening, timings: Timings)

var state: State          // computed on every read; see below
func begin()              // claims the alignment handler; call once, before any send
func end()                // idempotent: cancels the run, ticks off, handler released, keepAwake false
func send(_ intent: Intent)

enum Intent {
    case advance                       // the one gold action on any prompt: Continue, Play, Allow, I hear both, Measure, Try again, Open Settings
    case answerOnlyOne                 // the merged page's text action
    case adjustByEar, cancelRun, hearIt, checkAgain, dismissOffer, chainToNext
    case nudge(Int), revert, clearTuning
}

struct State {
    enum Stage { case prompt(Prompt), measuring(phaseLabel: String, fieldIsSounding: Bool), verdict(Verdict), fineTune(FineTune) }
    let stage: Stage
    let targetName: String
    let notice: String?                // a refused side command, cleared on stage change
    let keepAwake: Bool
    let measurementCount: Int          // the heavy impact's trigger
    let announcement: String?          // what VoiceOver says when a fact changed without a tap
}
struct Prompt { let title, body: String; let footnote: String?; let details: String?; let actions: [Action] }
struct Action { enum Rank { case gold, forward, quiet }; let title: String; let rank: Rank; let intent: Intent; let enabled: Bool }
struct Verdict { let headline: String; let milliseconds: Int; let ringsSettled: Bool; let hearItIsLive: Bool; let offer: Offer?; let chain: Chain? }
```

Behind the seam: the pages and their skips (`firstPreRunPage`, the brief's "The flow"), every copy table, the tick ownership, the per-run controller, the timers (lead-in, tail, apply timeout, the demo's 2 s and 4 s), the what-it-replaced capture, the in-sheet offer's dismissed flag, the chain. A pure `static func stage(for run: RunState, device: DeviceState?, permission: MicrophonePermission, replaces: Replaced?) -> Stage` does the derivation and is the test surface for every page rule; `send` and `handle` perform transitions and session calls; `stage` never has side effects. Events that must fire on a snapshot change (the Ready impact, the announcement) are keyed on derived Bools by the modifier, the way `:199-204` keys them on `ctaReady` today, so the model needs no snapshot callback.

Invariants and ordering. `begin()` before any `send`; a `send` before `begin()` is ignored. `end()` sends `setAlignmentTick(active: false)` if on, then `cancelAlignmentProbe` if staging, then releases the handler; safe to call twice. `.clearTuning` sends the tick off before the clear. A prompt has at most one `.gold` action, and it is the only action `.advance` reaches. `.advance` on Hold still sends the tick off, then starts the run. `.checkAgain` goes to Hold still with `walked` true (D10: no run without that page). A chain keeps `walked` and resets the verdict, the notice and what-it-replaced. A missing device yields a prompt with no actions. Nothing throws: every way a run ends without a number is a `.prompt` whose `details` carries the Mac's sentence and whose actions are Try again or Open Settings plus Adjust by ear; a refused side command is `notice` on the current stage. The Mac's verdict, not the phone's, names a first pass: `source` off the applied message. `keepAwake` is true while measuring, while Hold still waits on a precondition, and on a first-pass verdict until the clock is settled. `measurementCount` increments only on `.applied`, never on a refusal. `hearItIsLive` is false for `2 * timings.demoLeg` after the before-and-after is requested; `ringsSettled` turns true at `timings.demoLeg`, or at once when the Mac refuses the demo.

The view. `SyncSheet` keeps its `init(targetID:session:)`, holds the model in `@State`, and attaches `.syncRitual(model)`: a modifier that calls `begin` in `.task`, `end` in `.onDisappear`, sets `isIdleTimerDisabled` from `keepAwake` (false on disappear, unconditionally, as `:223` does today), attaches `.sensoryFeedback(.impact(weight: .heavy), trigger: measurementCount)` and the light impact on the derived Ready Bools, and posts `announcement` on change.

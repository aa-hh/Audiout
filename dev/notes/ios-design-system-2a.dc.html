1Audiouter Remote — Warm Signal under Liquid Glass

Read from your companion-app-phase2-ios worktree (4-tab SwiftUI shell, Snapshot protocol, DeviceRowView rules) and the Figma file's 405 variables. Everything below uses the real Warm Signal hexes — no invented colour.

1xThe reasoning — read this first
THE IDENTITY MOVE

Liquid Glass samples what sits behind it. So we don't tint the glass — we tint the room. The warm near-black canvas (#16130F → #1B1712) is the substrate under every glass layer, which means every sheet, tab bar and toolbar comes out warm on its own. Apple's utilities float over grey. Ours float over a warm-dark console.

FOUR RULES
Instruments sit ON glass, never inside it. A 1.6 pt halo ring dies under a blur. Rings, gold dots, meters and faders are always the top opaque layer.
Gold is scarce and means "armed". Selection, route-armed, meter hot end. Nothing decorative is gold — that's why an armed device reads instantly.
The micro-label voice carries over verbatim. SF Mono, 700, uppercase, +0.09 em: LIVE MUTED NO ROUTE. It's the one type detail nobody else's audio app has.
Light mode is Circuit, not a wash. Scaffolding aliases Circuit; instruments keep their authored Warm Signal values in both grounds. Flip the Appearance tweak.

ASSUMPTIONS I MADE
  • Tabs, in your order: Speakers · Apps · Groups · Connect.
  • Main Out never collapses — it's a fixed glass header, not a section.
  • Tapping a row arms/disarms it. Volume appears inline only once armed; everything deeper is behind the chevron.
  • PINNED is a curated zone above everything; below it, armed devices float to the top of their own section.
  • Collapsed sections remembered per Mac.

Why the sidebar pill stays the system's · AppKit draws it

A source-list sidebar draws its own pill in the system accent; AppKit never calls the row view's selection drawing there (NSTableRowView.h, and the trap recorded on PlateRowView). Two ways to a green pill, both declined: make green the whole app's accent through NSAccentColorName (every checkbox, pop-up, focus ring and the Settings sidebar turn green too, and only for people on the Multicolor accent), or leave the source-list style and draw the pill, losing the stock Show/Hide fold and the sidebar header styling.
